# Walkthrough poster

[poster.pdf](poster.pdf) and [poster.png](poster.png) are a one-page, step-by-step tutorial for first-time users: what Fiji does, how to set up this server, what to ask at each step, and what to check. The poster text and layout are covered by this repository's [license](../LICENSE); the logo and images keep their own terms.

The dots mark the centroids that `get_results` returned, one per counted particle.
